Need help?

You don't have to figure everything out alone. If you need a real person right now, these are genuine support services in Canada.

If you're in immediate danger

Call 9-1-1 or go to the nearest emergency department. If you can, stay with someone you trust and tell them what's happening.

9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline

If you're thinking about suicide, worried about someone else, or dealing with a suicide-related crisis, you can call or text 9-8-8 in Canada. Support is available 24/7.

CALL 9-8-8 TEXT 9-8-8

Kids Help Phone

Kids Help Phone provides support to young people across Canada. You can reach a crisis responder by text or talk with a counsellor by phone.

Text CONNECT to 686868 24/7 crisis text support. Call 1-800-668-6868 24/7 phone support. Visit Kids Help Phone Access their online support and resources.

This website is not a crisis service, counselling service, or emergency service. The people in the Late Night Line are other visitors, not trained crisis responders.
