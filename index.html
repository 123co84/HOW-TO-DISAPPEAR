Need help?

This page is intentionally different from the rest of the site.

If things feel too intense, you deserve a real person who can help.

If you're in immediate danger

Call 9-1-1 or go to the nearest emergency department. If possible, stay with someone you trust.

9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline

In Canada, you can call or text 9-8-8 if you're thinking about suicide, worried about someone else, or experiencing a suicide-related crisis. Support is available 24/7.
