Need help?

This page is intentionally different from the rest of the website.

If things feel too intense, you deserve a real person who can help.

If you're in immediate danger

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. If you can, stay with someone you trust.

9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline

In Canada, you can call or text 988 for crisis support. The service is available 24/7.

CALL 9-8-8 TEXT 9-8-8

Kids Help Phone

Young people in Canada can also reach Kids Help Phone for support.

About howtodisappear
